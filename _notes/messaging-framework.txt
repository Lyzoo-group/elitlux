07 · Recommendations
A few suggestions before final drafting
These small steps would help the messaging land even better once it goes live.
1 Sharing business contact details
Weʼd kindly suggest adding a UK address, email and phone number to the website.
It helps shoppers feel confident about the business behind the products.
2 Confirming product claims
It would be helpful to check a few phrases with the supplier before we use them,
such as anti-allergy, dust mite protection, “100% breathableˮ and “Britishˮ. Wording
around heating bills and pain relief may also need gentle softening, so every
message stays accurate.
3 Sharing a delivery timeframe
If possible, a specific delivery time (for example, “dispatched within 1 working
dayˮ) in place of “Fast Deliveryˮ would give customers clearer expectations, and
could become a useful message in its own right.
Once these points are confirmed, weʼll move on to final copy drafting for
the website and product pages.
