T H E B R A N D
Comfort is not a luxury
UK households who want their bed to feel like a hotel’s — home refreshers treating themselves, supported
sleepers (side sleepers, neck and back pain, expecting and nursing parents), and practical hosts furnishing
guest rooms and holiday lets.
Elitlux is aUK bedding brand founded on a simple belief: everyone deserves to sleep the way they
do in a good hotel — without paying hotel prices for it. We design duvets, pillows, protectors, toppers and
cushions that bring that feeling home, and that calm, caring, quietly premium mindset should show up in
every layout built from this kit.
