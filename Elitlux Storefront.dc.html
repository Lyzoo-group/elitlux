About Elitlux

Comfort is not a luxury.

Elitlux is a UK bedding brand founded on a simple belief: everyone deserves to sleep the way they do in a good hotel — without paying hotel prices for it. We design duvets, pillows, protectors, toppers and cushions that bring that feeling home.

Our values

What we stand for

  • Considered comfort

    Every product starts with how it feels: plump fills, smooth covers and quilting that keeps its shape.

  • Honest quality

    We tell you exactly what’s in it — the fill, the fabric, the size — and never promise more than a pillow can do.

  • Care in the detail

    Machine-washable fills and covers, so keeping your bed fresh is easy.

  • Restorative calm

    Hotel-quality comfort for every home, without the big spend.

Delivery & returns

Free UK delivery on every order, anywhere in the UK.
