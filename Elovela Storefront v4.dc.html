About Elovela

A hotel bed, brought home.

Elovela is a UK sleep and comfort brand making pillows, mattress toppers, protectors and support pillows that bring the feeling of a hotel bed home. We’re honest about what’s inside, easy to care for, and delivered free across the UK.

Our values

What we stand for

  • Soft by design

    Every product starts with how it feels: plump fills, smooth covers and quilting that keeps its shape.

  • Honest inside

    We tell you exactly what’s in it — the fill, the fabric, the size — and never promise more than a pillow can do.

  • Care built in

    Machine-washable fills and covers, so keeping your bed fresh is easy.

  • Fair to every home

    Hotel-quality comfort for every home, without the big spend.
